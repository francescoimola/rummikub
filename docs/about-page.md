## About

I come from a family of makers, artists, and business owners. As a child I dreamt first of becoming a magician, then of designing chairs. And while neither of those dreams came true, I didn’t land far off.

I now live in Folkestone, on the Kent coast, but I was born in Italy in a land called Ciociaria. These days, I spend my time [making complex stories a little less complex](/studio/), and drawing people closer to good products, good services and good talent.

I graduated in Digital Arts from the University of Greenwich, which is where much of this started, and then spent six years between cultural institutions and the Film & TV industry. I also [write](/writing/), and volunteer with [Open Art Folke](https://openartfolke.com) to support fellow artists. And I [make art](/art/) of my own, with words and pixels.

## Philosophy

We live in turbulent times. There is an overabundance of information, and in the midst of it I see designers taking on facilitator roles, aka helping people communicate.

When it comes to marketing, I specialise in the trust-based kind—or the art of building credibility and long-term relationships by being honest, helpful and consistent with your audience.

## About this website

This site is not merely a self-promotion machine, but a self-invention machine. An [island](https://notbadnotbad.substack.com/p/i-hope-someday-i-get-to-visit-your) to inhabit with my work, ideas, and the things I care about. Built for the long term, but malleable enough to change over time.

I've taken steps to make this island as engaging and accessible as possible. I hope that you'll enjoy your stay.
