Early days into co-producing and designing for Open Art Folke, Folkestone's first artist-led festival of open studios, exhibitions, performances, and workshops. This grassroots initiative celebrates the town's creative community and invites artists to expand beyond their studios into shop windows, streets, cafés and parks.

So far I’ve refreshed the organisation’s visual identity, helped shape the 2026 festival edition, and articulated what Open Art Folke is to its community. I’ll soon be involved in more graphic design work across digital and print channels.
