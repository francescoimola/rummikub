I built this website myself. The current iteration is the third or the seventh, I stopped counting to be honest. Its always-changing design and shape have kept me busy on many evenings and given me plenty of business and coding challenges from which to learn from. I immensely grateful to this website and, despite having spent way too much time on it, I don't have a lot to regret about it. 

## The current infrastructure is as follows

- Built with 11ty (eleventy) using VS Code, my own coding knowledge and the help of AI (Claude and Opencode)
- Pages are written either in Nunjucks or Markdown, with javascript propping some useful features (like only leading videos when the user is about to scroll near them)
- Code stored on GitHub
- Deploy with Cloudflare Plages. E

The design was drafted in Figma and then ported to the project relying in part of cleacss, a "CSS framework for enthusiasts", and in part of my own digging my own heels in layers of css, too many layers of css. 

## Yes, this site has a "history" 

This website was previously two websites! I used to have a container for everything in my practice related to business, and one for art slash everything else. I have since recognised the value of my multidisciplnary practice and have re-owned my name, my creativity and my broad skillset as what makes me unique. Coming from an arts background has become part of my "personal brand", if you wish, rather than being hidden away.

This realisation led to a major redesign of this website in 2025, which won the Public Awards for Best UI, UX and Innovation, plus a Special Kudos at the CSS Design Awards at the beginning of 2026. 

Later that year, I continued to update the site, which shape now sits even closer to what I imagined for my practice: a welcoming, non-prescriptive container flexible enough to accomodate all I do and have to offer, and smart enough to change form to meet the needs of all its audiences (potential clients and employers, curious folks, people who want to read what I post, and fellow designers, artists, or copywriters).
