I built this website myself. The current iteration is the third or the seventh, I stopped counting to be honest. Its always-changing design and shape have kept me busy on many evenings and given me plenty of business and coding challenges from which to learn from. I immensely grateful to this website and, despite having spent way too much time on it, I don't have a lot to regret about it. 

## The current infrastructure is as follows

- Built with 11ty (eleventy) using VS Code, my own coding knowledge and the help of AI (Claude and Opencode)
- Pages are written either in Nunjucks or Markdown, with javascript propping some useful features (like only leading videos when the user is about to scroll near them)
- Code stored on [GitHub](https://github.com/francescoimola/rummikub/)
- Updates handled either manually or using [Pages CMS](https://pagescms.org)
- Deploy with [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com) at francescoimola.com

## How it was built

The design was drafted in Figma and then ported to code. In terms of its styling, I relied in part on cleacss, a "CSS framework for enthusiasts", and in part on me own digging my own heels in layers of css, too many layers of css. 

Slowly, as you do, pages were aded, features were added, bugs started appearing and started getting fixed. After that, there came weeks of polishing, adding small features int he frontend but mostly slimming down a back-end that I never imagined would become so expansive. 

Because I am a web designer first, developer second, my knowledge of code—especially in the backend—has its limits. Thankfully, this site was redesigned in the Year of the Horse aka 2026 (which I have heard means those born this year are decisive but also easily swayed and impatient people, and I'm gonna lean into the stars by admitting that this can also be said of how this version of the website came to be). Anyway, back to the point, AI is very much a thing, and it is pretty much inevitable when it comes to coding as of today.

I relied on AI assistance to: 

- Port designs from Figma into good enough code that I then polished manually
- Improve accessibility
- Make the code I wrote more reliable
- Troubleshoot bugs 
- Write tests
- Connect this repo to Pages CMS

I am aware that using large language models carries a carbon footprint. Even though I could argue that francescoimola.com is built and maintained by a one-person team, and to some extent AI acts as my assistant—"saving energy" by cutting down on time spent fixing things or making me more productive—I don't want to justify my actions with that argument. And while this site’s code is top-class in terms of efficiency and weight, I acknowledge that such measures won't erase the environmental cost of using generative AI in the first place.

## Yes, this site has a "history" 

This website was previously two websites! I used to have a container for everything in my practice related to business, and one for art slash everything else. I have since recognised the value of my multidisciplnary practice and have re-owned my name, my creativity and my broad skillset as what makes me unique. Coming from an arts background has become part of my "personal brand", if you wish, rather than being hidden away.

This realisation led to a major redesign of this website in 2025, which won the Public Awards for Best UI, UX and Innovation, plus a Special Kudos at the CSS Design Awards at the beginning of 2026. 

Later that year, I continued to update the site, which shape now sits even closer to what I imagined for my practice: a welcoming, non-prescriptive container flexible enough to accomodate all I do and have to offer, and smart enough to change form to meet the needs of all its audiences (potential clients and employers, curious folks, people who want to read what I post, and fellow designers, artists, or copywriters).
